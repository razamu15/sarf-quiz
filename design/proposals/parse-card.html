What changed, and why

One card, every aspect

  • Five questions become one. tense, voice, doer and mood stop being separate questions drawing separate words. They are axes of one question about one word, plus a new form axis. This is تَحْلِيل صَرْفِي — the exercise a student is actually set.
  • Relevance is untouched. An axis still dies when the pool makes it constant, with the registry's own reason printed verbatim. Drop the muḍāriʿ and the Iʿrāb row leaves, exactly as the setup bar promised.
  • The chip, not the option. Five stacked 60pt AnswerOptions would be 1,000pt of screen. A chip already holds a bilingual label in fixed slots and already toggles.
  • Chips, not segmented controls — even for a fixed set like Tense. A segmented control has no empty state, and an answer control must be able to show “nothing chosen yet”.

The rule that now covers every row

  • Every reading the written form admits is correct. تَنْصُرُ is هِيَ and أَنْتَ; خِفْتَ is maʿrūf and majhūl; a dual muḍāriʿ conflates manṣūb and majzūm. D-20's doer rule becomes the general rule of the screen.
  • D-70's cost disappears. “A tap and Check on every voice question” was the price of always-checklist voice. There is now one Check for the whole card, so it costs nothing.
  • Iʿrāb is always shown, with a مَبْنِيّ option. If the row appeared only on a muḍāriʿ, its presence would answer the Tense row for free. Naming a māḍī as mabnī is a real judgement, so the leak becomes content.

What it costs

  • Bāb can no longer be asked. You read the bāb off نَصَرَ يَنْصُرُ — both tenses — and one conjugated word does not carry it. It stays in the sheet's explanation.
  • A word is right only if every row is. The session score counts words: 7 / 10. Partial credit lives in Results' By question, which still breaks down per axis.
  • The card is tall. Five rows plus a sheet does not fit at once on a small phone. Two things make it work: rows you got right collapse to one line, and the card opens scrolled to the first row with a miss. Without both, the sheet sits on top of the row you are meant to review.
  • Mood chips lose their explanatory tail. “marfūʿ — the default state” is an AnswerOption label; a chip gets “marfūʿ”. The tail moves to the answer sheet.
