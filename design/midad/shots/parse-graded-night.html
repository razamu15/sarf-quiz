تَنْصُرُ is فِعْل مُضَارِع مَعْلُوم مَرْفُوع, Form I, from نَصَرَ يَنْصُرُ — and it serves هِيَ and أَنْتَ alike. One written form, two pronouns.

  • The تـ prefix serves both هِيَ and أَنْتَ — only context separates them.
