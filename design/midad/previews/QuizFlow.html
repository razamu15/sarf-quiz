What is different

  • Feedback rises from the bottom. The word and the options stay on screen and Continue sits under the thumb. Today the feedback is appended below the options, so the page scrolls and the word you are studying leaves the screen.
  • The tick box says “checklist” before you tap. Today the doer question always reads “Select all that apply”, then submits on the first tap whenever the answer happens to be single — the instruction and the interaction disagree.
  • See the table opens over the quiz and closes back to it. Today that link leaves the quiz entirely (and the session's answers are lost with it).
  • Red is the sign, and it arrives only after the answer. Here: the governing particle on question 3, the diverging ḥaraka on question 4. Before the answer every letter is ink, because the sign is the answer.
  • Arabic runs are isolated in the explanation, so “the word — its label from its citation” keeps the order it was written in.
  • No raw ids. The card used to carry a chip reading doer; the question itself says what is being asked.
