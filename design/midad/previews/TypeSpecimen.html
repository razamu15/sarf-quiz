Why Scheherazade New

Scheherazade NewChosen. SIL, drawn for fully vowelled text: big, well-spaced marks and few ligatures.

لَمْ يَنْصُرُوا · مُسْتَسْلِم · مُدَّ · اُنْصُرْنَ · أَنْتُمَا
Noto Naskh ArabicThe fallback. Cleaner at small sizes, shorter lines — but the marks are lighter, which is what we are asking people to read.

AmiriBeautiful, and wrong here: it stacks يستخرج into a vertical ligature, so the letters a learner is trying to identify pile on top of each other.
