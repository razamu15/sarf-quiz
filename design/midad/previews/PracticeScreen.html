What is different

  • The consequence is under your thumb. The count, what this setup asks, and what the last tap changed sit in a bar pinned to the bottom. Today they are at the very end of a long scroll, below the controls that change them.
  • One axis, one control. Tense, voice and iʿrāb are three labelled groups, not a two-axis grid — a grid says everything at once but has to be learned before you can change one thing. The two facts it carried are kept: the iʿrāb group sits inside the muḍāriʿ's rule and stays visible (disabled) when the muḍāriʿ is out, and the line underneath counts the whole charts the three add up to.
  • The length sits with Start. Every control above decides what can be asked and moves the count; the length decides how many of them you want, which is a different kind of choice — so it is in the bar, beside the button, not a fourth picker in the body.
  • A retired question says why it retired. “Iʿrāb — iʿrāb needs the muḍāriʿ in more than one state” is QUESTION_RULES' own reason string, printed verbatim; it names the axis to widen, which a struck-through chip cannot (PRODUCT_SPEC §5.2b). The classic Practice screen has said this since A2 and it is worth keeping.

Worth knowing

  • Classic Practice is frozen verbatim for the practiceFlow experiment. Restyling one flow and not the other would decide the comparison by other means — so either apply the tokens to both flows at once, or land this after the flag resolves.
  • A plan is tense × voice × iʿrāb, so a single chart still cannot be picked on its own — “māḍī majhūl plus muḍāriʿ maʿrūf” is not expressible and the controls do not pretend otherwise. That keeps QuizPlan exactly as it is.
