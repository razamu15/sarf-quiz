What is different

  • The chart is a grid, not a list of fourteen. Singular, dual and plural across; he, she, you (m), you (f), I down — the shape of the paper chart a student already owns. The endings line up in columns, which is what makes a pattern visible.
  • No “View table” step. The pickers stay at the top and the chart under them updates as you tap; today you choose, tap View table, then come back out to change one axis.
  • Gaps are shown as gaps. Pick majhūl on an intransitive verb and the row says so instead of offering an empty table.
  • The amr grid shrinks to six cells rather than padding eight empty ones.

Needs a decision

  • PRODUCT_SPEC §5.6 specifies “all 14 rows, vertically scrollable”. The grid replaces that. The list stays available as the Dynamic Type fallback — at the largest accessibility sizes three columns of vowelled Arabic cannot fit.
  • Colouring the affixes by column (the thing that makes a chart teach) needs the engine to return prefix / stem / suffix. That export has to exist before the corpus freeze (ROADMAP B3), like waznRoot.
